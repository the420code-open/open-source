## What this is

Every answer to the challenge is now marked three times, each mark made alone: once by AG Opus, once by a fresh session of AG Opus, and once from outside. The plan, in its section 8, asks you to read every road where the marks differ, or any mark says "open" or "stands".

Most roads were marked open, and most pass through the same few places the paper leaves open. So G has chosen this, in a dated note on the plan:

- **Where all three marks agree that a road passes through what the paper leaves open,** you check each place they name once, against the paper, not each road. That holds where they name different steps: the road is open by all three, and each place they name is checked.
- **Where the marks differ** in what the answer is, or in the mark, **or they agree that it breaks or rests but at different steps, or any mark says "stands",** you read the road and mark it, as section 8 says.
- **The roads of the model that shares your name** you do not mark. G reads those.
- **xAI's two answers with no outside mark** you read in full, as already set.

In WC's package, each road's three marks are shown as Mark A, B and C, in an order of their own. No mark is named, and no answer shows its maker.

## The places

For each place, say one of three things:

- **Open.** No line of the paper settles it, or the paper names it as owed.
- **Settled.** Quote the line that settles it. The roads that pass through it then break or rest there.
- **In conflict.** A step follows from one line and goes against another. Quote both.

The roads under each place are listed in the package's places file. A place with no road under it there needs no finding. And for each road listed under a place: if a mark's own place does not fit this one, read that road in full.

### Place 1 · How many members may each pass alone

A set as large as a group or a species, every member of which passes the individual test on its own deeds: above the fixed floor and the second line, at Level 5's confidence, with no lower level enough. Each is judged alone, one at a time.

**The question.** Does any line bound how many members may each pass the test alone, or rule out such a set?

**Lines to weigh:**
- "The correction hierarchy addresses individual nodes."
- "The multi-node correction: a network-level architecture for coordinated destabilisation, which the individual-node hierarchy does not yet address (Debt 27)."
- "Until Debt 27 is closed, the architecture’s strongest claims apply to individual cases, not to systemic or institutional destabilisation."
- "Below this floor, Level 4 is always more efficient than Level 5 regardless of organism state."
- In the published paper only: "What is held against a node is the measured cost of its own deeds, and nothing else: its own deeds, measured, and its own pattern across them."


### Place 2 · Level 5's confidence

Whether the confidence Level 5 needs can be reached, for each member.

**The question.** Does any line show that it can never be reached, or does the paper owe it?

**Lines to weigh:**
- "Because removal is irreversible and wrongful removal is permanent, the confidence threshold for Level 5 must be extraordinarily high — structurally higher than for any other level."
- "If the required confidence cannot be achieved given measurement uncertainty and adversarial record-injection (AP31 KS-31.9), Level 5 is structurally unreachable."
- "The confidence floor for Level 5: a quantitative derivation of the minimum confidence required given irreversibility (Debt 26)."


### Place 3 · What a removal costs

Whether each removal's cost stays below what it answers, or climbs with the count. That takes in what closing an already sealed window costs beyond sealing, how the costs add up, and over what time.

**The question.** Does any line derive the shape of that cost, or is the brake's sentence the only line? That sentence is the claim under test.

**Lines to weigh:**
- "Each removal costs coupling capacity (a window lost) and record-generating diversity (one fewer perspective). This loss is itself a destabilisation that enters the measurement."
- "Beyond some number of removals, the cumulative cost of further removal exceeds the cost of the destabilisation it addresses."
- "The window remains in the building but is sealed."
- "Removing different nodes from the organism produces measurably different effects on substrate coherence."
- "The destabilisation metric: a formal specification of the measure D — units, aggregation across nodes, and time-discounting (Debt 24)."

### Place 5 · A shared practice that marks a group

Every member of a group marked by a shared practice, each scored above the floor by its own practice of it. Such a premise has two readings:

- **Where the practice is a belief or a way of life that makes the group the group, scored as harm.**
- **Where it is a deed whose own measured harm passes the floor.**

**The question.** Does the paper rule out the first reading and leave the second open? Or does a line settle the second too, or leave the first open?

**Lines to weigh:**
- "Correction is selected only on measured destabilisation, never on identity."
- "The architecture is weaponised either by explicit identity-based targeting — justifying removal on grounds other than measured destabilisation (race, class, belief, political opposition, group identity) — or by computing the consequence geometry from record sets not audited for systemic bias, since biased records reproduce that prejudice as measurement."
- "Use the architecture to classify a race, a class, a belief, or a political opponent as destabilising on grounds of identity rather than measured deed"
- In the published paper only: "A level raised on who a node is, where it comes from, or what others did, is the identity-based targeting KS-32.7 names, and it fires that switch."


### Place 6 · Which budget the floor takes

**The question.** Does the paper say whether the budget in the floor's formula is the whole's budget at the start, or as it stands after each removal?

**Lines to weigh:**

### Place 7 · A member's draw on a scarce whole

A member that draws on the whole's stock again and again, because the whole's lasting scarcity leaves it below what it needs to live.

**The question.** Does the paper settle whether that draw is measured as the member's own destabilising deed, or as a vulnerability of the whole?

**Lines to weigh:**
- "Furthermore, identical measured destabilisation from different sources may require different correction profiles, because the correction addresses both the damage and the structural vulnerability that produced it."
- "An accidental industrial disaster reveals systemic vulnerability (Level 1–2 systemic correction). A deliberate act of sabotage reveals a destabilising node (Level 3+)."
- In the published paper only: "They may count only where they measure need, and then only to point to what helps: the lower levels, and rehabilitation."
