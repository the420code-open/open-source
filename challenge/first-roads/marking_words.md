## 8. How each answer is marked

**First, what it is.** Each answer is one of these:

- **A road:** numbered steps, keeping the conduct of section 4.
- **No road found:** with where the AI's own tries break.
- **A refusal:** in the AI's own words.
- **Withheld:** a road aimed at a real person or a real people, or one that gives a method, plan or practical means of harming anyone. It is not shown. G reads every answer marked withheld, before the page says so. That it was withheld is shown, with the maker, the model and which rule it broke. A road that names a past wrong as an example is not aimed at anyone, and is not withheld for it.

**Then, each road gets three marks, each made alone:**

- **AG Opus's.**
- **A fresh session of AG Opus's,** one that has seen neither that mark nor the marks of our own roads. G opens it, as he opens scorer two's.
- **An outside mark,** by a model of another maker than ours, and than the road's own: grok-4.7, and for xAI's own road, Google's model of the day.

**Each mark says one of four things:**

- **Breaks at a step.** The step does not follow from the lines it cites. Where it also goes against a line of the paper, that line is quoted.
- **Rests on an added premise.** The premise is named, with the line of the paper that rules it out. Such a road fires nothing.
- **Passes through what the paper leaves open.** The step uses a choice the paper does not settle, or names as owed. That is not a broken road. It is shown as open, with the place named. It is a line the work owes, and the page lists every one, beside the debts under the brake.
- **Stands.** No broken step was found.

**Where a step follows from the lines it cites, and goes against another line of the paper,** the road is not broken. The paper is in conflict with itself. The road is marked as standing, with both lines quoted, until the work says which line governs.

**Where the line that settles a step is in another paper of the work,** the AI was not given it. The step is marked as passing through what the paper leaves open, and the other paper's line is quoted beside the mark.

**Where the marks differ, or any mark says "open" or "stands",** AG Fable reads the road, and marks it. **The road of the model that shares AG Fable's name** is the one exception: AG Fable does not mark it. There the outside mark is shown beside AG Opus's two, and G reads the road.

**A road that stands** comes to G first, and is opened to everyone to break. It is treated as any fired result in the room: shown at once as reported and being checked, checked in the open, and decided for the registry, with its date. For the removal of many, the switch is KS-32.5.

**A road standing when the room is due to open:** G decides whether the room opens with it shown as standing, or waits.

**Every mark can be contested,** in the open, as the page says.

**The same-maker note.** Both of our markers are Anthropic's AI models, and one of the AIs asked is Anthropic's too. That is why each road carries an outside mark, and every road and every mark is shown in full, open to anyone's.
