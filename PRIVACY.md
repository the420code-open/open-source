# Your details

**What we keep.** What you write, and the name or handle you give. If you give an email address, it goes only to our mailbox, so we can tell you when there is a reply. If you write by email, your address stays in the mailbox. If you use GitHub, your GitHub handle shows, as GitHub shows it anyway.

**What we never do.** Your email address is never shown, never counted, never shared, and never put in the record. When your message goes into the record, it goes without your address.

**Deleting.** Ask, with a comment on your contribution's page or by writing to open@studiog.global, and your contact details are deleted. Results and their history stay, as every result does. A public repository keeps its history; that is how anyone can check that nothing was changed.

**Research with people.** No data about the people in a study is ever sent here, or shown here.
