What you are looking at. A small world, built from the work’s own rules: AP02 The Operator, and the two machines of the One Awareness page. Each circle is an operator: anything that has to keep itself going, in AP02’s words “a person, a bacterium, a corporation, a civilisation”. The bar beside it stands for its corridor; the more it holds, the more it can still do. Every operator leaks a little all the time. Nobody here can top itself up alone. It lives on what its links give it.

Three kinds of operator. A cooperator gives on its links, and cuts a link that costs it more than it brings. A cooperator that never cuts keeps giving, whatever comes back. A defector takes, and gives nothing.

What the sliders do. How much each operator gives. What giving costs. How fast everything leaks. How much a gift is worth to the one who receives it. And how much chance and noise there is.

The page’s charger. The two machines from the One Awareness page, with the page’s own numbers. Move the slider, and watch where the defector stops winning.

What this world can show, and what it cannot. It shows what the paper’s rules do, when anyone runs them. It cannot show what people will do. And it proves nothing on its own: the claims it tests were written down and fixed before any run, and the results go on the record, whichever way they fall.
