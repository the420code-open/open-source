# Conduct

This place exists to test a body of work, and to build on it. Disagreement is the point. Criticism of the work, however hard, is welcome, and it is never a reason to decline anything.

**Not welcome:** harassment or abuse of anyone; material that is dangerous, or unlawful; results that are made up; work presented as someone else's, or as the work's own when it is not; the same thing posted again and again after an answer.

**What happens.** Something that breaks these rules is withheld or declined, with the reason given. The count of withheld items is public. Their content is not. A person who keeps breaking them is no longer answered, and is told so once.

**To contest a decision,** comment on your contribution's own page. It is read again, and the answer is dated.
