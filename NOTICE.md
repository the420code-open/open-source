# What is the work, and what is built

**The 420 Code** is the books, the papers, the pages of its sites, and the Master Kill Switch Registry. It is published under Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0. Free to share. Never altered. None of its files are copied here. They are quoted, and linked.

**Everything built here** is free, with no restriction. Texts and data: CC0 1.0 Universal. Code: Zero-Clause BSD or MIT, at your choice. Studio G gives both away, and owns nothing of what others build on them.

**The floor** is CC0, but many of its lines are the work's own words. Those lines are marked. Quote them. Never alter them. Each version of the floor carries a fingerprint, so anyone can check that a copy is unaltered.
