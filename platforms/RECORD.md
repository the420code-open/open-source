# The Embryo on other platforms · the record

Where the Embryo is listed, where it is not, and why. The home stays aiethics.global and this repository. Every copy elsewhere says so at the top, links back, and carries its version and fingerprint. The work goes up whole, or not at all (G, 13 November 2026: "The work is never adapted to pass any authority, or to be listed anywhere… whole, or not at all.").

Each platform's terms were read from its own public pages on 13 November 2026. The notes, with every page, quote and date, are kept with the desks' records.
