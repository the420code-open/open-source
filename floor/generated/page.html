F6.12 One Awareness, "The second road". the420code.org/one-awareness/

Now set two corridors side by side. What you do moves what I can still do. Two machines, each with a charger only the other can crank. Each holds 100 units and burns 12 an hour staying on; cranking costs the cranker 4 an hour and delivers 10 to the other. Both crank: each runs down at 12 + 4 − 10 = 6 an hour, and both live sixteen hours and forty minutes. One stops cranking. Its gain is real — 4 an hour, kept. Its partner, paying 16 and receiving nothing, dies at six hours and fifteen minutes. The defector is taking 10 and paying nothing, so it runs down at 2 an hour, and is left with 87.5 units and nobody to turn the handle, burns 12 an hour alone, and dies at thirteen hours and thirty-two minutes. The defection was worth 4 units an hour. It cost three hours of being.
